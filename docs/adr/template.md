# NNNN. Title as a decision, not a topic

- **Status:** Proposed | Accepted | Superseded by NNNN
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD

## Context

What was true that forced a choice. Include the failure that prompted it, if there was one -
a concrete symptom is worth more than a paragraph of principle.

## Decision

What was decided, in the present tense. One or two sentences.

## Consequences

What this costs, and what it buys. Be specific about the cost: a record that lists only
benefits is a record nobody believes.

## Alternatives considered

What else would have worked, and why it was not chosen. This is the section that stops the
decision being re-litigated, so name the option someone will propose.
